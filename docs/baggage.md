# Cloudhopper Airlines: Baggage Policy

Each passenger may bring one personal item and one carry-on bag free of charge. Carry-on bags must fit in the overhead bin and weigh no more than 35 pounds.

The first checked bag costs $35 and the second checked bag costs $45 on all domestic flights. Checked bags may weigh up to 50 pounds. Bags between 51 and 70 pounds incur an overweight fee of $100.
