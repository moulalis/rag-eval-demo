# Cloudhopper Airlines: Cancellations and Refunds

You can cancel any booking within 24 hours of purchase for a full refund, as long as the flight departs at least 7 days later.

After the 24-hour window, refundable fares can be cancelled for a full refund to the original payment method. Non-refundable fares can be cancelled for a travel credit, valid for 12 months from the date of cancellation.

Refunds are processed within 7 business days.
